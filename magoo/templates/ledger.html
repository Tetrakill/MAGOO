How this is computed

Recomputed from the stored ESI feeds on every load; nothing is saved here. Units sold and revenue come from wallet sale transactions (the truth — a filled order already produced its fills, so order history only explains outcomes) plus finished item-exchange contracts you issued: a contract holding only pipeline finals credits its whole price to them (split by cached quote when it holds several); a contract that bundles other items or asks for items in return counts its hulls as units only. Net deducts estimated sell fees by where the hull sold (the sale's station or structure, from ESI): at an NPC station the high-sec hub rates — broker {{ "%.2f"|format(broker_rate * 100) }}% · sales tax {{ "%.2f"|format(sales_tax * 100) }}% · SCC {{ "%.2f"|format(settings.capital_scc_surcharge * 100) }}% · freight to Jita {{ settings.freight_out_isk_per_m3|isk }}/m³; at a structure the {{ settings.structure_market_label() }} rates — broker {{ "%.2f"|format(settings.capital_broker_rate * 100) }}% · tax {{ "%.2f"|format(settings.capital_sales_tax * 100) }}% · SCC {{ "%.2f"|format(settings.capital_scc_surcharge * 100) }}% · freight {{ settings.structure_freight_in_isk_per_m3|isk }}/m³ for sub-caps. Capital-class hulls (capitals, freighters, jump freighters, Orca) take the flat movement cost of {{ settings.capital_movement_cost_isk|isk }}/hull at either venue — they fly themselves; Keepstars, Palatine Keepstars and Sotiyos pay no per-m³ term at all. Any structure other than {{ settings.structure_market_label() }} is charged its rates too (the only structure rates in Settings); a contract ESI gives no location for falls back to the Planning tab's class rule. Contracts pay the same estimates, as an approximation. Cost per unit is the lag-based cost of the latest executed index run that built the product (a deleted pipeline loses its run history); cost of goods sold costs each sale at the latest priced run executed before it (a run planned before any price pull prices nothing and is passed over; a sale made before any priced run had been executed takes the earliest priced one), so executing a new run never re-costs an earlier sale, and net income is net proceeds minus it — products without an executed run show revenue but no net income. Unrealized revenue is what your open sell orders and outstanding contracts are listed for — price × units, before fees, like Revenue — hangar stock is not counted. Sales to your own characters or corporations and unpriced contracts are listed but never counted; auctions are not read at all. A character's corporation is read as its current corporation; a character switched off on the ESI tab also stops carrying the corporation-wallet sales only its own feed reports.
