Alchemy

How alchemy is planned

When reaction slots are left over, cheaper Unrefined reaction routes substitute for direct composite reactions — you run the unrefined formula, then manually reprocess its output into the composite plus recovered inputs. A contended reaction pool disables alchemy (direct reactions are far more slot-efficient).

Skills — how fast your jobs run

How skills apply

Entered here, not read from ESI. Industry −4%/level and Advanced Industry −3%/level apply to all manufacturing; Reactions −4%/level to reactions; the last four apply −1%/level only on blueprints that require them — Outpost Construction on Upwell structures, Standup modules, Skyhooks and structure fighters. Tech I structure rigs and structure components need only Industry; Tech II Standup rigs also take the Tech 2 Science level.

High Sec Trade Hub Pricing

The high-sec hub (Jita 4-4 in the price region): sub-capital hulls, Upwell structures, structure rigs/modules and structure components sell-quote here and list at its NPC station with these fees; bought inputs default to its quote. Null Sec Trade Hub Pricing

What the null-sec hub (structure market) is used for

One null-sec structure market (the C-J6MT Keepstar by default) serves two jobs. Buying: every input the plan buys is priced at whichever is cheaper landed — Jita price + Jita freight-in, or this market's best sell price + the C-J6 freight-in below; the Buy list badges C-J6 buys and flags shallow ones whose sell ladder has fewer units beating the Jita landed price than the plan buys (the best price is still used; buy the rest in Jita). Pipeline finals are never compared. Selling: capitals, freighters, jump freighters, and Orcas sell-quote here instead of the price region, with the fee pair below and the fixed movement cost in place of ISK/m³ freight-out. Needs a character logged in with the structure-markets scope and docking access. Upwell structures are NOT priced here — they use the high-sec panel above.

Build settings — where you build each class of item

How rig bonuses are applied

Where each class of items is built, and what rigs the structure carries. Rig bonuses: ME T1 −2.0% / T2 −2.4%, TE T1 −20% / T2 −24%, scaled by the rig family's security band — engineering rigs ×1.0 highsec / ×1.9 lowsec / ×2.1 nullsec, reaction rigs ×1.0 lowsec / ×1.1 nullsec (reactions cannot run in highsec). Thukker rigs (component classes and the structures class) are lowsec specialists: ME −3.7% on capital components / −2.0% on other components and on structures, TE −20%, all scaled ×1.9 in lowsec but only ×0.1 anywhere else.
