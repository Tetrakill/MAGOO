Alchemy — cheaper reaction routes for reaction slots that would sit idle

How alchemy is planned

Some composites can be made two ways: the direct reaction, or an "unrefined" reaction whose output you reprocess yourself to get the composite plus some of the inputs back. A direct reaction makes far more per slot, so alchemy only uses reaction slots no direct job can use this cycle — free slots, and the slots of jobs your stock can't start yet. In those slots the plan uses the unrefined route wherever it works out cheaper than the direct reaction, or than buying a composite the plan would otherwise purchase — never units your jobs need this cycle, because reprocessed output arrives at the end of it.

Compressed Sourcing — buying compressed ore and gas to reprocess yourself

How compressed buying is planned

For each group switched on below, whenever the plan buys those raw materials it also considers buying compressed ore, compressed moon ore or compressed gas and reprocessing it. A compressed buy pays for every material it yields: moon ore bought for its moon materials also covers the minerals it produces, even with Minerals switched off.

Each candidate is priced by walking the cheaper market's sell orders for the quantity you would actually buy, delivery included, so one small cheap order can never win on its own. It is chosen only when it is clearly cheaper than the raw materials it replaces. Purchases are rounded up to whole reprocessing batches; anything produced beyond this cycle's needs is left-over stock that counts for nothing in the decision (though the reprocessing tax is still charged on it). Compressed items already in your hangars are ignored, and ice is not covered.

Needs game data downloaded with version 1.25 or later. If the Buy list never shows a compressed row, download the game data again from the Dashboard.

Skills — how fast your jobs run, and your invention chance

How skills apply

Entered here; they are not read from the game. Industry cuts manufacturing time by 4% per level and Advanced Industry by 3% per level; Reactions cuts reaction time by 4% per level. The remaining four cut time by 1% per level, but only on blueprints that require them: Outpost Construction on Upwell structures, Standup modules, Skyhooks and structure fighters. Tech I structure rigs and structure components need only Industry; Tech II Standup rigs also use the Tech 2 Science level.

Invention chance uses three of them: the racial Encryption Methods level (one fortieth per level) and the two datacore sciences (one thirtieth per level each): Racial Starship Engineering for ship-engineering datacores and Tech 2 Science / Engineering for everything else.

High Sec Trade Hub Pricing — Jita: where inputs are bought and most products are sold

The high-sec hub is the main station of the price region (Jita 4-4 in The Forge). Sub-capital hulls, Upwell structures, structure rigs and modules and structure components are priced for sale here and listed at its NPC station with the fees below. Bought inputs are priced here unless the null-sec market is cheaper once delivery is included. With the current values: broker fee {{ "%.2f"|format(broker_rate * 100) }}% · sales tax {{ "%.2f"|format(sales_tax * 100) }}%. Check them against a sell-order window in the client.

How the pricing basis works

Ladder walks the sell orders for the quantity you would actually buy, so a large buy pays the real average rather than the cheapest order's price, a buy can split between the two markets, and whether to build or buy an intermediate is decided unit by unit: the units the market sells for less than they cost to build are bought, the rest are built. Min Sell Order prices any quantity at the cheapest sell order. Max Buy Order prices any quantity at the highest buy order, which is what you pay when you place your own buy orders and wait. Switching to or from Max Buy Order needs a price refresh, because the refresh pulls that side of the order book.

Null Sec Trade Hub Pricing — the structure market: cheaper inputs, and where capitals are sold

What the null-sec market is used for

One null-sec structure market (the C-J6MT Keepstar by default) does two jobs. Buying: every input is priced at whichever market is cheaper once delivery is included: the Jita price plus the Jita courier rate, or this market's price plus the courier rate set below. Both markets' sell orders are walked together, cheapest delivered order first, so one buy may split between them, and Multibuy lists each market's share. When the stored orders run out before the quantity is filled, the row is badged unsourced: the rest is priced at the last order used but belongs to no market and appears in no Multibuy block (unless Jita's stored list was cut off at 300 orders, in which case the rest is assumed to sit deeper in Jita). Your final products are never bought. The Pricing Basis below chooses between walking this market's sell orders and pricing any quantity at its best sell or buy order, as it does for Jita.

Selling: capitals, freighters, jump freighters and Orcas are priced for sale here rather than in the price region, with the fees below and a fixed movement cost per hull instead of a courier rate. This needs a character logged in with the structure-markets scope and docking rights. Upwell structures are not priced here; they use the high-sec panel above.

Build Settings — where each class of item is built, and with which rigs

How rig bonuses are applied

For each class of item: the structure it is built in, the security of that system, the rigs fitted, the system cost index and the facility tax. Material rigs save 2.0% (T1) or 2.4% (T2) of materials; time rigs save 20% (T1) or 24% (T2) of job time. Each bonus is multiplied by the rig family's security factor: engineering rigs ×1.0 in high-sec, ×1.9 in low-sec and ×2.1 in null-sec; reaction rigs ×1.0 in low-sec and ×1.1 in null-sec (reactions cannot run in high-sec). Thukker rigs, available for the component classes and for structures, are low-sec specialists: materials 3.7% on capital components and 2.0% on other components and structures, time 20%, multiplied by 1.9 in low-sec but only by 0.1 anywhere else.
